Tradition

01 — Our Story

A Heritage Woven in Devotion

For generations, পূর্ব বদকুল্লা বুড়িমা বারোয়ারি has been the beating heart of our community's Sharadiya celebration. What began as a humble neighbourhood gathering has grown into a cherished tradition — where art, faith and togetherness meet under the golden glow of the dhaak.

Every year, the courtyard comes alive with the fragrance of shiuli, the rhythm of the dhaak, and the warmth of familiar faces returning home. This is more than a puja — it is our shared inheritance.

02 — previous Year

A Theme Rooted in Light

Sharadiya 2025 draws on the imagery of the eternal flame — a mandap built from terracotta, brass and hand-painted alpona, honouring the artisans of Nadia whose craft has shaped this pandal for over four decades.

Every motif, from the arch to the aanchol of Maa's saree, is chosen to tell one story: that devotion, however old, can still feel newly lit each year.
